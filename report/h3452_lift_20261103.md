## An exact decomposition of the remaining equations

Write H = Z2^2 x Z5 and G = H x Z43. For the six size-seven multiplier classes C_j,
let c_ij(h) be the sign of block i on h x C_j, and let e_i(h) be its sign at (h,0).
The saved compression fixes

    y_i(h) = e_i(h) + 7 sum_j c_ij(h).

Because there are six class signs, their sum is even. Consequently y modulo 14
determines e uniquely. Direct computation for this certificate gives

    sum_i PAF(e_i)(h) = 80 at h=0,
                       -12 at h=(0,u), u != 0,
                        4 when the Z2^2 coordinate is nonzero.

For every lift, at lags (h,0), the full correlation is exactly

    sum_i PAF(x_i)(h,0) = sum_i PAF(e_i)(h)
                          + 7 sum_{i,j} PAF(c_ij)(h).

Therefore the six cyclotomic columns, summed over the four blocks, must satisfy

    sum_{i,j} PAF(c_ij)(h) = 480 delta_0(h).

This is a necessary part of the full lift equations; it is not an additional
assumption. `logs/h3452/lift_decomposition.json` records the computed e arrays.

## The complementary projection and its compatibility

Compress along H instead of along Z43. All compressed entries are even. After
division by 2, the four integer sequences on Z43 must have summed PAF 863 delta_0.
Their values at zero are fixed as (2,3,3,1), and their row sums as (2,3,3,29).
The six other values per block are constant on the cyclotomic classes represented
by 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, respectively.

An exact solution was found in 59.59 seconds:

| block | at zero | C1 | C2 | C3 | C6 | C7 | C9 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 2 | -4 | -1 | -1 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| B | 3 | 2 | -2 | 1 | 1 | -1 | -1 |
| C | 3 | 2 | 2 | 0 | -2 | -2 | 0 |
| D | 1 | -2 | 0 | 5 | 4 | 1 | -4 |

The squared sum of the six-column entries is 120; hence the zero-lag energy is
23 + 7*120 = 863. Every nonzero lag was also checked directly to be zero.
These are integer compressed sequences, not sign sequences or a Hadamard matrix.

To satisfy both projections, each block's 20 x 6 sign table has specified row
sums from y and specified column sums equal to twice the table entries above.
A binary transportation model found compatible tables. Rectangle switches of
the form (+,-;-,+) <-> (-,+;+,-) preserve both margins, and therefore both
projections. This compatibility was checked on actual expanded length-860 sign
sequences, not inferred only from the separate projection certificates.

## Algorithms, scope, and validation

- `h3452_exact_lift.py`: Boolean sign variables, exact AND linearizations of pair
  products, and exact cell-cardinality constraints. Supports full feasibility and
  squared-residual minimization on selected cells. Four random assignments per
  model are checked against direct PAF evaluation. A planted satisfiable target
  is accepted; a fully fixed invalid candidate is rejected. A local OPTIMAL status
  means the restricted residual minimization was solved, not that a core exists.
- `h3452_tabu.py`: evaluates all whole-cell replacements of the correct
  cardinality, with tabu exclusions, aspiration, and fibre-preserving perturbations.
  Every saved score is recomputed directly on all 860 lags.
- `h3452_prime_gate.py`: 24 integer variables for the complementary projection,
  with exact quadratic PAF equations. It uses independent Z43 arithmetic.
- `h3452_double_fibre.py`: constructs compatible sign tables and searches with
  rectangle switches. One hundred legal rectangle deltas are checked against
  direct PAFs before searching. Every checkpoint verifies both projections.
- `h3452_validate_lifts.py`: independently reconstructs cyclotomic orbits and
  group translations, verifies all saved residuals, and checks both projections.
  Output: `logs/h3452/lift_validation.json`.

The new runs used Python, numpy, numba, and OR-Tools. The exact full model and
limited local repairs are not exhaustive searches of the fibre. The heuristic
failures and the 60-second UNKNOWN status provide no nonexistence certificate.

repairs. The separate two-projection branch is available for structured searches
